BRAN

Named for the Raven King — the smartest bird, the darkest omen. A tiny masked-diffusion language model that doesn't write left-to-right. Every generation begins as pure noise, a blank field of [MASK] tokens, and text crystallizes out of it as the model unmasks the most confident tokens in parallel, step by step. Watch it happen below, or give it <mask> spans to infill.
