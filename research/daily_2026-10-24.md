## 1. CGAU novelty double-check (the queued 2026-10-23 pick)

- **Lookahead Unmasking / LookUM** (Lee et al., arXiv:2511.05563, ICML 2026,
  KAIST/Berkeley/KRAFTON) — the closest adjacent work found. Reformulates
  sampling as *path selection* over candidate unmasking orders; a verifier
  (sequence-certainty measure, fully unsupervised) steers away from local
  errors. 2–3x compute cost; +4 pts HumanEval/GSM8K, +8 MBPP on LLaDA.
  Boundary vs CGAU: LookUM *selects among candidate paths* with a
  coherence verifier — it has no rollback-to-cache recovery, no proof
  certificates (semantic contracts), and no re-masking of committed
  tokens. CGAU gates each commit on certificate-extendability and recovers
  by rollback + targeted re-masking. Same problem family, different
  mechanism. Absorb the "local errors cascade irreversibly" diagnosis
  (their Figure 1 is the exact motivation for our recovery leg); do not
  copy path-selection.
- **LAVE re-read** (arXiv:2602.00612, ISSTA 2026, via yesterday's notes +
  today's search snippets): Algorithm 1 confirmed — propose token,
  lookahead-verify, accept/reject, `c_fail` counter, cache-enhanced
  recovery at threshold τ. All at the CFG *syntax* level (JSON/C++/SMILES;
  syntactic@1 66.0% → 98.7% on SMILES/LLaDA-8B). CGAU's recovery lifts
  this *pattern* to semantic proof state; the verifier and the cache
  contents differ completely. Inspiration, not a port.
- **UnMaskFork** (test-time scaling via deterministic action branching) —
  assumes *monotonic* unmasking ("tokens are never re-masked", per the
  review). CGAU's backtracking explicitly violates this assumption; the
  two are incompatible by design, which is itself a differentiator.
- **RL unmasking policies** (arXiv:2512.09106, LLaDA/Dream) — learned
  policies, correctness + compute-penalty reward. No certificates, no
  guarantees. Orthogonal.
- **"A diffusion model picks its answer a fifth of the way through"**
  (arXiv:2608.05687, via groundtruth.day) — commitment order is causally
  important in MDLM math reasoning; window-style samplers fix a reasoning
  pathology. Independent evidence that *gating the unmasking order*
  (CGAU's whole thesis) matters. Supports, doesn't compete.

**Novelty verdict:** the triad (certificate-margin early exit +
proof-state rollback recovery + reason-targeted re-mask backtracking at
the semantic-certificate level) has no published prior art found.
CGAU proceeds as a potential XIIV invention. The per-placement *gating*
itself is CGMS's (in-repo) — the doc states this plainly; the invention
claim is the triad, not the gate.
