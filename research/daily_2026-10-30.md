## Part A — research findings (2024-2026, fresh ground; Oct 27 covered
EB-Sampler / DEMASK / Info-Gain / UPO / self-speculative dLLM decoding)

1. **NAVIRA** (arXiv:2606.06031, Jun 2026): decoupled stochastic
   remasking for masked diffusion LMs. A first forward pass estimates
   token quality and selects unreliable positions; those are masked out
   and a SECOND forward pass recomputes generation logits from the
   corrected context, so tokens marked for correction cannot
   contaminate their own regeneration. Remask targets are sampled from
   a temperature-controlled distribution over quality scores
   (diversity preservation, no entropy collapse). Inference-only, no
   retraining. Fit: STRONG -- directly addresses irreversible
   decoding errors, the exact failure family behind the assembled 1B's
   repetition/glitch generation. Port risk: its signal is
   model-estimated quality; XIIV's thesis wants proof certificates.
2. **R^2D -- Random Remask-Denoise** (arXiv:2610.30744, submitted
   22 Oct 2026 -- 8 days old): MDM inference as a Markov chain on the
   clean sequence space; random remask-denoise kernel, purely
   test-time, finetuning-free. Near-log-linear test-time scaling on
   logical tasks: >97% hard Sudoku, >99% 3-SAT; beats LLaDA2.1-mini
   HumanEval+. Fit: notable for the Adaptive games track (verifiable
   rewards), but it is a raw scaling procedure, not a XIIV invention;
   needs many iterations (expensive at 1.67 tok/s CPU).
3. **Constrained decoding for dLLMs via finite automata**
   (Dang & Ermon, arXiv:2607.07026, Jul 2026): exact, tractable
   sampling from the constrained mean-field posterior under ANY
   finite-automaton constraint; guarantees satisfaction by
   construction; compatible with parallel/block-wise decoding under
   arbitrary remasking schedules; depth linear->logarithmic via
   arithmetic-circuit depth reduction. Dream-7B BFCL-Live: greedy
   63.9% -> 71.5%, stochastic 22.3% -> 69.0% (<5% overhead). Fit:
   STRONG -- this is constrained decoding built FOR diffusion LMs
   (not ported from AR like XGrammar/Outlines), and it is the formal
   backbone for "guarantee by construction" in XIIV's decode stack.
4. **RDD -- Reversible Diffusion Decoding** (arXiv:2602.00150):
   stagnation detection + backtracking to earlier blocks via cached
   states + confidence-guided re-masking. Fit: same family as CGAU's
   rollback; no new thesis.
5. **Repetition literature**: HF `no_repeat_ngram_size` (AR-only logit
   masking, blunt); "detect and retry" (cut back to last clean point);
   repetition-dropout in training data (data-side, needs retraining).
   None are dLLM-native.

## Part A verdict -- today's pick
**NAVIRA's decoupled + stochastic remasking, lifted from
model-quality scores to proof-certificate signals, under a
no-repeat-n-gram certificate** (regular language -- Dang & Ermon's
finite-automaton framing). Novel surface vs the cited prior art:
NAVIRA gates remasking by estimated quality; ours gates by a
checkable certificate breach (repeated n-gram) and certifies the
final canvas repeat-free; HF's n-gram blocking is AR logit-masking,
inapplicable to parallel non-monotonic unmasking where repeats emerge
from simultaneous commitments and must be repaired by re-masking.
CPU-implementable (sampler-only), testable against pre-registered
falsifiers on scripted models. This is the day's invention candidate;
it earns the name only via the falsifier suite below.

## Part B -- implemented: DCMR (`xiiv/dcmr.py`, `tests/test_dcmr.py`)
Decoupled Certificate-Margin Remasking. Certificate: the
position-ordered committed canvas (masks as separators) contains no
repeated n-gram of order n (default 4). Mechanisms:
- Per-commit no-repeat gate: a token is committed only if it does not
  complete a repeated n-gram against the current canvas (sound: a
  repeat needs two complete equal windows; the second to complete is
  always rejected).
- Suspect scan: positions participating in a repeat (certificate
  breach) or committed under conf_floor (NAVIRA-style unreliability).
- Decoupled regeneration: suspects are masked, then a SECOND forward
  pass recomputes logits from the cleaned canvas (remasked tokens
  cannot contaminate their regeneration); taboo on the offending
  token breaks re-commit churn.
- Stochastic selection: remask targets sampled without replacement
  from softmax(suspicion / temperature) -- no repeated hammering,
  diversity preserved.
- Fail-closed: unsatisfiable instances stall out (completed=False);
  a completed canvas violating the certificate asserts loudly.
- `policy="vanilla"` baseline (confidence unmasking, no gate) built
  in for the falsifier comparisons.

Falsifier results (scripted repeater model, CPU, deterministic):
- F1 certificate soundness: PASS -- final canvas has zero repeated
  4-grams; gate fired 3x (non-vacuous).
- F2 diversity: PASS -- distinct-4-gram ratio 1.000 vs vanilla 0.077.
- F3 pass budget: PASS -- 4 forward passes in 4 steps (<= 2x steps,
  structural); on the repeater the gate alone sufficed (0 remasks),
  same pass count as vanilla.
- Extra: determinism (same seed -> identical ids), frozen positions
  never remasked, unsatisfiable instance fails closed (completed=False,
  no repeats emitted), flat-distribution run fires the regen pass and
  terminates, preexisting prompt repeat recorded not hidden.
- Suite: 10/10 new tests pass; 33/33 incl. CGAU/EB-gate/OpenAI-server.

Also wired as opt-in CLI: `python -m xiiv.sample generate --dcmr`
(prompt tokens frozen, never remasked). Explicitly experimental:
tested on scripted models only, NOT evaluated on a real checkpoint
(RAM-blocked today; owed before any quality claim).
