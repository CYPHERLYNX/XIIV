**THE ONE PICK -- Certificate-Guided Early Commit (CGEC).** Fuses
Prophet's confidence-gap early-commit + Mündler-style CFG constraints
on diffusion decoding + XGrammar-style incremental grammar checking,
and -- the novel part -- emits a machine-checkable certificate per
generation (tokens + grammar-state trace + min confidence gap),
replayable by an independent verifier WITHOUT model weights. Prophet
commits without a parser, Mündler constrains without certifying
confidence, XGrammar is AR-only; the diffusion x grammar-certification
x emitted-certificate intersection is original and documented as a
XIIV invention. Pure sampler code, zero GPU, runs on the 1B today,
measurable immediately. Composes with contracts.py and gives the
Proof Machine's 8 arithmetic primitives a certified decoder as its
first grammar domain.

## Part B — implemented: CGEC (xiiv/cgec.py, xiiv/cert_verify.py)

- `xiiv/cgec.py`: block-wise decoding (block=32); per step, one
  forward pass, then (1) Clover-style parallel fill of positions with
  top-1 prob >= tau (0.95), (2) Prophet-style all-in one-step fill
  when every remaining masked position in the block has top1-top2 gap
  >= gamma (0.10). Every commit is gated on EXACT feasibility: the
  position-ordered committed subsequence must remain completable to a
  full valid arith-v1 expression given the remaining masks, checked by
  forward/backward PDA reachability tables (O(L) per table build;
  analytic transition inversion, brute-force cross-checked exact).
  Fail-closed: infeasible canvas, even length (arith-v1 needs odd
  length -- every complete expression is odd-length, proved in the
  module), or stall -> completed=False, never a half-certified emit.
- `xiiv/cert_verify.py`: independent verifier -- its own PDA
  implementation, replays the commit trace checking prefix-validity +
  completability at every step, re-parses the final canvas, recomputes
  min_gap, checks counters. Tampering with tokens/symbols/order/gaps
  breaks at least one named check.
- `arithmetic_alphabet_32k()`: builds the domain alphabet from the
  real 32k tokenizer with round-trip verification. MEASURED: digits,
  +, -, /, (, ) are single tokens; `*` is ABSENT from the vocab
  entirely, so `x` (id 79) is the multiply key -- documented, and the
  helper asserts if the vocab ever gains `*`.
- CLI: `--cgec` flag on `generate` (mutually exclusive with --dcmr,
  not wired into scratchpad/refine/trajectory -- same guard pattern).
- Tests `tests/test_cgec.py`: 15 tests, pre-registered falsifiers
  F1-F5 all PASS, KILL not triggered:
  - F1: scripted valid model -> completed, final == target
    expression, independent verifier accepts.
  - F2: saboteur (top-1 always grammar-invalid at all 7 positions)
    -> completes via second-best candidates, 6 gate rejects, final
    still the valid target.
  - F3: confident scripted model -> all-in fires at step 1:
    1 forward pass vs vanilla's 7 on the same plan, identical output.
  - F4: four tamper modes (tokens, symbol, gap, dropped commit) all
    rejected with named failures.
  - F5: PDA battery (6 valid / 11 invalid incl. wildcard masks).
- Full suite: 264 passed (no regressions).
