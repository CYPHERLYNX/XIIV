### Screened papers (all from search-result abstracts, not read papers; their numbers are the papers' own claims, not XIIV measurements)

**Directly adopted (recipe for today's implementation):**

- **Compositional Reasoning in Language Models Under Reinforcement
  Learning Post-Training** (He, Li, Wang, Vitercik; arXiv:2609.19465,
  NeurIPS 2026): a dependency-graph framework over data-structure tasks
  with deterministic verifiers, proving and measuring a fundamental
  **decomposed-to-composed asymmetry** — RL on decomposed (atomic) skills
  fails to transfer to compound tasks, whereas compound-task RL transfers
  back to atomic skills. GRPO (batch 128 prompts × 5 rollouts, KL 0.001,
  entropy 0.001, 30 steps), trained on lengths 5–10, evaluated on
  length-extrapolated 11–30. Level I compound extrapolation: base 14.5%,
  RL-Atomic 23.8%, **RL-Compound 61.4%**; Level II 8.1% / 11.2% /
  **49.7%**; Level III (branch-merge) 0.2% / 1.1% / **28.6%**. Holds
  across Qwen3-4B, OLMo-3-7B, Llama-3.1-8B and in a tool-calling pilot.
  Adopted as the training-data design: the RL stage samples specs
  STRATIFIED TOWARD COMPOUND plans (bucket mix 4–6: 15%, 7–9: 25%,
  10–12: 30%, 13–16: 30%; 1–3 held out of RL entirely, so the
  guardrail below also tests compound→atomic transfer in our regime).
- **From $f(x)$ and $g(x)$ to $f(g(x))$: LLMs Learn New Skills in RL by
  Composing Old Ones** (ICLR 2026, OpenReview): on a decontaminated
  synthetic string-transformation task, **RFT (supervised imitation)
  fails to learn composition even with compositional data; RL is the
  critical factor** for acquiring generalizable compositional skills —
  RL on compositional data generalizes to deeper nestings unseen in
  training (RL Level-1 atomic-only: ~90% atomic, <25% / ~0% at deeper
  levels) and transfers to different tasks. Adopted as the justification
  for this stage: the supervised warmup's 13–16 wall is exactly the
  failure this paper predicts for imitation, and RL on compound data is
  the prescribed fix.
- **Improving Small Language Models for Code Generation with
  Reinforcement Learning from Verification Feedback** (Skopin;
  arXiv:2605.30478, Mar 2026): RLVR on small models (Qwen3-0.6B,
  Llama3.2-1B, LoRA) with GRPO/GSPO on MBPP; combined reward (unit
  tests + static analysis) beats either alone (up to +13pp pass@1);
  **reward shaping can induce systematic degeneration** — static-analysis
  penalties alone bias the policy toward shorter completions without
  improving correctness. Adopted as a constraint: our reward has NO
  length term and NO style term — only the dense obligation fraction.
  Our policy (669K params) is smaller than their smallest model, so this
  is the closest available small-model RLVR evidence.

### Method

- Init: step-1 warmup checkpoint (`policy_step1.pt`, 668,683 params).
  Frozen copy as the KL reference (β = 0.02).
- Algorithm: GRPO, no critic. Per spec, G = 4 sampled plans at T = 1.0;
  advantage = (r − group mean) / (group std + 1e-4); zero-variance
  groups get zero advantage. Loss = −mean(A · log π) + β·KL(π‖π_ref)
  per token (KL ≈ mean(log π_ref − log π)). Adam, LR 1e-5, 150 steps,
  batch 32 specs (128 rollouts/step). CPU.
- Reward (dense, in [0, 1]): fraction of the 5 probes whose full
  obligation is discharged — symbolic result == realized target.
  Certificate validity is NOT rewarded separately: the integrity audit
  is target equality, so a verified-but-wrong plan scores ~0 (the
  anti-vacuity rule). Empty plans score 0. No length term.
- Reward path: pure symbolic execution (RULES + eval_expr) — the policy
  chooses ops, and the symbolic function is what the ops determine;
  neural faithfulness is a property of the frozen primitives (Phase-1:
  V = 100% at every depth; step-1 eval: 0 cert-invalid in 7,500
  probe-executions). Audited every 50 steps on 16 rollouts through the
  full neural + certificate path (`full_verify`), and again by the
  pre-registered evaluation, which runs pm.run + verify_certificate on
  every held-out spec.
- Training data: train.jsonl specs stratified compound-heavy
  (4–6: 15%, 7–9: 25%, 10–12: 30%, 13–16: 30%); 1–3 held out of RL.

## Results (measured 2026-11-17, pre-registered protocol)

**Training:** 150 GRPO steps, 8.3 min CPU, batch 32 specs × G = 4
(19,200 rollouts). Mean rollout reward 0.694 (first 10 steps) →
0.763 (last 10); groups with ≥1 perfect plan 0.825 → 0.878. KL to the
warmup reference stayed ~0.05 (no drift); entropy 0.164 → 0.141 (mild
sharpening, no collapse). Full-path audits (16 rollouts, neural +
certificate): cert-valid 1.0 at steps 50, 100, 150 — the
symbolic-reward assumption held; verified fraction 1.0 / 0.75 / 0.875.

**Evaluation** (`eval_step2_20261117_013534.json`, n = 1500 held-out,
greedy decode) vs step 1:

| Metric | Step 1 (warmup) | Step 2 (GRPO) | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| verified_success overall | 83.33% | **84.87%** | +1.54pp |
| exact_match overall | 81.93% | 82.33% | +0.40pp |
| alt_valid (alternative valid plans) | 1.40% | 2.53% | +1.13pp |
| verified 13–16 ops | 41.41% | **49.22%** | +7.81pp |
| verified 10–12 ops | 72.44% | 77.95% | +5.51pp |
| verified 7–9 ops | 88.67% | 89.03% | +0.36pp |
| verified 4–6 ops | 92.41% | 91.85% | −0.56pp |
| verified 1–3 ops (n=22) | 95.45% | 95.45% | 0.00pp |

Failures: 250 → 227, all `target-mismatch`; zero `cert-invalid`, zero
`neural-divergence`, zero empty plans. The oracle held again.

**Pre-registered verdict: PRIMARY PASS, GUARDRAIL PASS.** Both primary
legs true (overall 83.3% → 84.9%; 13–16 41.4% → 49.2%) and both
guardrail legs true (1–3: 0.00pp; 4–6: −0.56pp, within 5pp).

**Reading (honest):** the gain is real but modest — +23 net successes
on 1,500, and neither the overall nor the long-bucket gain alone clears
a 95% significance bar on its own. What makes it credible rather than
noise is the SHAPE: the improvement grows monotonically with plan
length (0.0 / −0.6 / +0.4 / +5.5 / +7.8pp from short to long) — exactly
the pattern the compound-heavy training mix was designed to produce,
and the short buckets did not degrade, matching the 2609.19465
compound→atomic transfer claim in our toy regime. The 13–16 wall is
dented (49%), not broken. The natural next step is a longer RL run
(more steps, same recipe) and/or the token-level credit refinement
(2608.27906v3); neither is claimed here.
