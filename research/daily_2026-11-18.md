### Method (same recipe as step 2; only the step count varies)

- Init: step-2 checkpoint (`policy_step2.pt`, 668,683 params). The KL
  reference is the init checkpoint, as in step 2 — i.e. each stage
  anchors to its own init; β_KL = 0.02 unchanged.
- GRPO, no critic: G = 4, T = 1.0, LR 1e-5, batch 32 specs
  (128 rollouts/step), 500 steps, seed 20261118. Same compound-heavy
  stratified mix (4–6: 15%, 7–9: 25%, 10–12: 30%, 13–16: 30%; 1–3
  held out of RL). Dense reward = fraction of the 5 probes whose full
  obligation is discharged (execute + certificate verifies + symbolic
  result == realized target); no length term. Full neural + certificate
  audit every 50 steps. CPU (~28 min expected; step 2 took 8.3 min for
  150 steps).

| Metric | Step 2 | Step 3 | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| verified_success overall | 84.87% | **85.47%** | +0.60pp |

| alt_valid | 2.53% | 3.67% | +1.13pp |

| verified 10–12 ops | 77.95% | 78.35% | +0.40pp |
| verified 13–16 ops | 49.22% | **49.22%** | **0.00pp** |

**Pre-registered verdict: PRIMARY FAIL — PLATEAU. GUARDRAIL PASS.**

- Primary leg 1 (overall improves vs 84.87%): true, +0.60pp — but a
  two-proportion z-test gives z = 0.46, not significant; +9 net
  successes on 1,500 is noise-level.
- Primary leg 2 (13–16 improves vs 49.22%): FALSE. The bucket is
  literally identical — 63/128 verified in both step 2 and step 3.
  The step-2 long-bucket gain (+7.81pp) did **not** compound with 350
  more GRPO steps.
- Guardrail: 1–3 unchanged, 4–6 +0.93pp (within 5pp); zero
  cert-invalid; audit cert-valid 1.0 throughout. Pass.

**Honest reading:** the response-level GRPO recipe saturates at ~85%
overall / ~49% at 13–16 ops. More steps of the same recipe buy
nothing at the long end — the wall step 2 dented is not moved by
step-count alone. This is a negative lever for "longer GRPO", reported
as such per the pre-registration, not re-framed as progress. One real
signal inside the flat top line: exact_match fell (−0.53pp) while
verified rose (+0.60pp) and alt_valid rose (2.53% → 3.67%) — RL is
moving the policy away from planner imitation toward alternative valid
compositions, but not toward more *correct* long plans. The 13–16 wall
is a capability wall for this reward/policy scale, not a training-time
wall.
